U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs took in about $176 million on Oct. 27, led by roughly $151 million into BlackRock's IBIT, after $110 million the session before, for a two-day total near $286 million. Spot ether ETFs lost about $149 million over the same two days. [Source: Bitcoin.com News](https://news.bitcoin.com/bitcoin-etf/bitcoin-etfs-add-176m-as-2-day-inflows-top-286m)
