category: Europe

Britain's Financial Conduct Authority intends to require decentralised finance businesses to be authorised under the crypto regime that starts on Oct. 25, 2027, the Financial Times reported on Thursday. Jane Moore, the FCA's head of payments and digital assets, pointed to how quickly stolen funds from the Oct. 29 Binance hack moved through DeFi protocols.

The regulator is also weighing a rule that would keep UK retail stock orders, including tokenised shares, on regulated venues. Its consultations on DeFi guidance closed on Nov. 18, with final guidance expected in January.
