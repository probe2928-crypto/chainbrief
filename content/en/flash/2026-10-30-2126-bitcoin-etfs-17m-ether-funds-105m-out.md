U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs took in a net $17.2 million on Thursday, a fourth straight day of inflows but far below Wednesday's $137 million, as the Binance hack hit prices. Ether funds lost $105.5 million, led by $71.7 million out of BlackRock's ETHA, and Solana products shed $27.7 million, according to Farside data.
