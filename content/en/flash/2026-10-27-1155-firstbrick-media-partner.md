CHAINBRIEF is the media partner of First Brick Foundation, a foundation in formation that plans to support children's education in Southeast Asia, starting with learning support and growing toward classrooms and schools. The foundation says it will record every gift and payout on a public blockchain once it is registered, and is not taking donations yet. CHAINBRIEF will report its progress. [Source: First Brick Foundation](https://probe2928-crypto.github.io/firstbrick/)
