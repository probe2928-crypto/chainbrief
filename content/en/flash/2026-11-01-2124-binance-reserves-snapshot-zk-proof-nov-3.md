A Binance proof-of-reserves snapshot taken at 00:00 UTC on Oct. 31 shows customer balances in BTC, ETH, USDT, BNB, SOL, USDC, XRP, USD1, RLUSD and ZEC fully covered, the exchange said. A complete zero-knowledge proof is now expected by Tuesday, Nov. 3, a day later than the Nov. 2 date given earlier. Binance has also promised a detailed incident report once its investigation ends.
