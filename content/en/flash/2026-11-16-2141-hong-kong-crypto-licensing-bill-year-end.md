---
title: Hong Kong plans a bill by year-end to license crypto dealers, advisers, managers and custodians
time: 2026-11-16T21:41:00+09:00
category: Regulation
important: false
---
Financial Secretary Paul Chan told the Bloomberg Global Regulatory Forum on Monday that the government will send legislation to the Legislative Council before the end of 2026. It would extend Hong Kong's licensing regime beyond trading platforms to virtual asset dealing, advice, asset management and custody, under a "same activity, same risk, same regulation" principle.
