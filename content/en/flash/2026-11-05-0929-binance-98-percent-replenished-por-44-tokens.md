title: Binance says 98% of hacked assets replenished, widens reserve snapshot to 44 tokens
time: 2026-11-05T09:29:00+09:00
category: Exchanges
important: true
---
Binance said in its seventh incident update that it has refilled about 98% of the assets hit in last week's hack from its own funds, and will buy the last 2% of thinly traded tokens on the open market and over the counter. A new proof-of-reserves snapshot taken at 00:00 UTC on Nov. 4 covers 44 tokens, up from 10, with the full zero-knowledge proof expected to take 50 to 60 hours. Co-CEO Richard Teng put the amount frozen or intercepted so far at about $2.85 billion.
