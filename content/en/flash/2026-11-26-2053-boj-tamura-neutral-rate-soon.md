Bank of Japan board member Naoki Tamura said on Thursday the central bank needs to bring its 1.25% policy rate up to a neutral level soon, and should be ready to raise faster if inflation looks set to overshoot its 2% target.

He pointed to Middle East oil prices, swings in the yen and AI-driven demand as upside risks to prices. The dollar traded around 159 yen on the day.
