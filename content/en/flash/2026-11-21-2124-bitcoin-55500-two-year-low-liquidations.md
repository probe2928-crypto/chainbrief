---
title: Bitcoin hits about $55,500, its lowest since September 2024, as liquidations top $2.4 billion
time: 2026-11-21T21:24:00+09:00
category: Bitcoin
important: true
---
Bitcoin slid to roughly $55,500 early Saturday, below the July 1 low near $57,700 and the weakest level in more than two years, before bouncing back toward $58,000. CoinGlass data put 24-hour futures liquidations at about $2.4 billion to $2.5 billion, close to nine-tenths of it from traders betting on higher prices. The largest single wipeout was a roughly $52 million BTC/USDT position on Binance.
