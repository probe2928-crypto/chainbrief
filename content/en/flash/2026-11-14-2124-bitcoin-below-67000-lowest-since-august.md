category: Bitcoin

Bitcoin fell under $67,000 late Friday U.S. time and kept sliding into Saturday, with CoinGlass showing a 24-hour low near $66,430, its weakest level since August and roughly 29% below where it stood a year ago. About $1.23 billion in leveraged positions were liquidated over the 24 hours to Saturday, around 83% of them bets on higher prices, according to CoinGlass figures cited by Altcoin Buzz. Ether slipped under $1,900 and solana under $76. [Source: CryptoTicker](https://cryptoticker.io/en/bitcoin-price-below-67000-lowest-since-august/)
