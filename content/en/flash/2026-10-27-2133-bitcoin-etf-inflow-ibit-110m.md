time: 2026-10-27T21:33:00+09:00
category: US

---
U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs recorded net inflows of about $110.3 million on Oct. 26, ending two straight sessions of outflows. BlackRock's IBIT was the only fund with new money, while the other products reported flat flows. Spot ether ETFs went the other way, losing roughly $88.6 million on the day.
