title: Hong Kong market maker Auros is reportedly winding down
time: 2026-11-27T13:45:00+09:00
category: Exchanges
region: crypto
important: false
---
Auros, the Hong Kong crypto trading and market-making firm founded in 2019, is winding down operations, CoinDesk reported, citing two people familiar with the matter. Neither the reason nor a timeline has been disclosed, and Auros has not responded to requests for comment.

Order books on venues and tokens where Auros quoted prices could thin out until other market makers step in.
