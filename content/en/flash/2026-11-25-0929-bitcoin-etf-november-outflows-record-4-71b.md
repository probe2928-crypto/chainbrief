title: US spot bitcoin ETFs set monthly outflow record at $4.71 billion
time: 2026-11-25T09:29:00+09:00
category: Bitcoin
important: true
---
November net outflows from US spot bitcoin ETFs have reached about $4.71 billion through Monday's session, topping June's previous monthly record of roughly $4.5 billion, according to Farside Investors data. Monday alone saw about $570 million leave, the fifth straight day of withdrawals, with BlackRock's IBIT accounting for $509 million.
