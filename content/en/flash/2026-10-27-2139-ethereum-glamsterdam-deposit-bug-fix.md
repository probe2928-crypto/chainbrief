---
title: Ethereum developers merge Glamsterdam fix to keep validator deposits away from builders
time: 2026-10-27T21:39:00+09:00
category: Altcoins
important: false
---
Ethereum's consensus specifications now include a fix, merged Oct. 26, for a Glamsterdam flaw that could have credited a pending validator deposit to a block builder registered under the same key. The change, proposed by a Lido engineer, sorts deposits by withdrawal credentials; it had been shelved on Oct. 19 before being reopened. No mainnet date is set, and client teams still have to implement and test the new rule. [Source: Unchained](https://unchainedcrypto.com/ethereum-developers-merge-glamsterdam-fix-to-keep-validator-deposits-out-of-builder-accounts/)
