The New York Attorney General's office has won a $1.37 billion judgment against Cynthia and Eddie Petion, founders of crypto trading firm NovaTech, over what the state called a Ponzi scheme.

The couple left the United States before a 2025 default ruling and have not been located, which makes collecting any of the money uncertain.

The state asked the court for the sum in October; many of NovaTech's investors came from Haitian-American church communities in New York.
