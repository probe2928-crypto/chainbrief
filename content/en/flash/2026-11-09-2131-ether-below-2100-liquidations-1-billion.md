title: Ether slips under $2,100 as crypto liquidations top $1 billion in a day
time: 2026-11-09T21:31:00+09:00
category: Altcoins
important: true
---
Ether briefly fell below $2,100 on Sunday, its lowest level since August, and was down about 5% on the day into Monday, while bitcoin touched roughly $73,700 before steadying near $74,500. CoinGlass data showed more than $1 billion of leveraged positions wiped out over 24 hours, about $913 million of them longs, with ether accounting for roughly $450 million. U.S. spot ether ETFs lost about $365 million last week, on top of $1.28 billion out of bitcoin funds. [Source: crypto.news](https://crypto.news/crypto-week-ahead-bitcoin-price-us-inflation/)
