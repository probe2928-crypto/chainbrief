The 11th Singapore FinTech Festival, co-organised by the Monetary Authority of Singapore, runs Nov. 18-20 at Singapore Expo, with a technology track on tokenisation, digital money, agentic AI and quantum computing. The invitation-only Insights2040 meetings that precede it, held Nov. 16-17, include a session on what is holding back the growth of tokenised markets.
