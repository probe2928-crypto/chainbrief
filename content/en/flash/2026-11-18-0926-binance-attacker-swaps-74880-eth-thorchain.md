---
title: Binance attacker swaps 74,880 ETH for bitcoin through THORChain
time: 2026-11-18T09:26:00+09:00
category: On-chain
important: false
---
Wallets tied to the Oct. 29 Binance breach moved 74,880 ETH, about $136.8 million, over roughly three hours on Nov. 17 and swapped it for bitcoin via THORChain, according to Lookonchain. One address flagged by MistTrack, idle since Oct. 30, split 34,279.66 ETH across three new wallets. The Justice Department has said more than $2.7 billion of the stolen funds is frozen, with court approval to seize or restrain over $1.2 billion.
