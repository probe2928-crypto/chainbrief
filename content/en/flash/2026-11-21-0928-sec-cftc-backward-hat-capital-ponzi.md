The SEC and CFTC sued Backward Hat Capital and its two co-founders in federal court in the Southern District of Indiana, alleging the firm raised about $560 million from more than 2,000 investors by pitching an "AI-driven" currency trading algorithm. A judge approved an asset freeze, and Manhattan federal prosecutors filed parallel criminal charges that put the total raised above $600 million.
