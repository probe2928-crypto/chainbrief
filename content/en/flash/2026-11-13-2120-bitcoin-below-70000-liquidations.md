---
title: Bitcoin slips under $70,000 for the first time since August as liquidations top $1.3 billion
time: 2026-11-13T21:20:00+09:00
category: Bitcoin
important: true
---
Bitcoin dropped below $70,000 late Thursday U.S. time, its lowest level since Aug. 19, and touched $67,555 on Coinbase before steadying near $68,600, according to CoinDesk and Unchained. CoinGlass data put futures liquidations at more than $1.3 billion over 24 hours in CoinDesk's count, $677 million of it in bitcoin, while a later CoinGlass snapshot cited by Unchained reached about $1.65 billion, nearly nine in ten of them long bets. The dollar index climbed above 103 for the first time since April 2025 after a hotter October CPI report, and ether fell under $2,000.
