title: Hotter October CPI sends bitcoin to its lowest level since late August
time: 2026-11-11T09:25:00+09:00
category: Macro
important: true
---
U.S. consumer prices rose 0.4% in October from September, above the 0.3% economists expected, and core prices rose 0.3% against a 0.2% forecast. Bitcoin fell below $74,000 after the release and touched about $72,643 on Coinbase, its lowest since late August, according to Forbes, while ether dropped 3.7% to about $2,042, The Block reported. CME FedWatch put the odds of a quarter-point hike at the Dec. 9 Fed meeting at about 85%, with the rest on a half-point move, after Governor Christopher Waller said he backs a December hike.
