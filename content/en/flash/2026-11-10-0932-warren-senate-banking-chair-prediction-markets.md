Sen. Elizabeth Warren, one of crypto's harshest critics in Congress, will chair the Senate Banking Committee when Democrats take control in January. A committee aide on Monday listed prediction markets, artificial intelligence and accountability among her priorities and said Trump family crypto businesses, along with tech executives and administration officials, should preserve documents.
