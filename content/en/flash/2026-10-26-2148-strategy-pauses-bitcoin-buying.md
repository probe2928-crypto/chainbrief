Strategy reported no bitcoin acquisitions for the Oct. 19–25 period in its Monday filing, leaving its holdings unchanged at 848,517 BTC. The pause follows a 267 BTC purchase the prior week, funded mostly through share sales, alongside a $193.1 million buyback of its STRC preferred stock. The company reports quarterly results on Oct. 29. [Source: The Crypto Times](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/10/26/strategy-discloses-no-bitcoin-purchase-while-stock-mstr-price-remains-in-focus/)
