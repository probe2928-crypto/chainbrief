---
title: French Senate rejects plan to make holders declare self-custodied crypto above €100,000
time: 2026-11-27T09:27:00+09:00
category: Europe
region: eu
important: false
---
France's Senate voted down amendments to the 2027 budget that would have required anyone holding €100,000 or more of crypto outside a service provider to declare it to the tax authorities. The government and the Senate finance committee both opposed the measure.

Senators also rejected a tax on swaps into MiCA e-money stablecoins and a plan to extend France's exit tax to crypto. The Senate is due to finish voting on the budget's revenue section on Saturday, with a final vote on the full bill set for Dec. 9.
