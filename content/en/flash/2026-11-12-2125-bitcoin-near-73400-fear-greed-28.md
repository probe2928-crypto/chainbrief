Bitcoin traded around $73,400 to $73,500 on Thursday morning UTC, down about half a percent on the day, after closing Wednesday near $74,015, according to The Crypto Desk and Ted Pillows' daily market reports; ether was just above $2,040. Both reports put the Crypto Fear & Greed Index at 28, in "fear" territory. Coinspeaker tied the slide to a sharp drop in Nvidia shares and MSCI's decision to drop Strategy from its indexes.
