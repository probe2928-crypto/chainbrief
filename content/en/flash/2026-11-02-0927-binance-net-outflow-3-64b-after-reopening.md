---
title: Binance sees $3.64 billion net outflow in first 9.5 hours of reopened withdrawals
time: 2026-11-02T09:27:00+09:00
category: Exchanges
important: true
---
Customers pulled about $4.65 billion from Binance between 06:00 and 15:30 UTC on Sunday while depositing about $1.01 billion, a net outflow of roughly $3.64 billion, the exchange said on X. That is about 2.1% of the $171 billion shown in its Oct. 28 proof of reserves. All 15 networks in the first phase were open by Sunday evening; the rest are still being tested.
