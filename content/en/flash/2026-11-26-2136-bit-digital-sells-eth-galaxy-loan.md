Nasdaq-listed Bit Digital sold 29,825 ether between Nov. 15 and Nov. 23 at an average price of about $1,676, the company said in an SEC filing on Wednesday. It used about $50 million of the proceeds to halve its secured loan from Galaxy to $50 million.

After the sales the company still held about 134,500 ETH as of Nov. 23.
