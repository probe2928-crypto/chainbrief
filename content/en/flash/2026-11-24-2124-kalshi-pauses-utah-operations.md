---
title: Kalshi pauses operations in Utah while CFTC's bid to block state enforcement is pending
time: 2026-11-24T21:24:00+09:00
category: US
important: false
---
Kalshi has paused operations in Utah, telling The Block and the Salt Lake Tribune on Monday that it would wait for a federal judge to rule on the CFTC's request to stop Utah from enforcing its gambling laws against the company. Judge Robert Shelby ruled in August that federal commodities law does not override Utah's gambling ban as applied to Kalshi, and the state has since filed criminal charges against the company and its founders. Kalshi said more than 177,000 Utah residents use the platform; the pause came the same day a New York judge declined to halt it there.
