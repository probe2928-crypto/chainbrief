Intercontinental Exchange said on Oct. 29 it will run a new market and clearing structure built on Hyperliquid for Trade[XYZ]'s perpetual futures on crypto, stocks, commodities and currencies, aimed at KYC-verified U.S. retail traders, and will take a strategic stake in Trade[XYZ]. The plan needs regulatory approvals that have not yet been sought, and no launch date was given.
