title: Russia lets non-qualified investors buy bitcoin, ether and USDT from Tuesday, within an annual cap
time: 2026-11-16T21:37:00+09:00
category: Regulation
important: false
---
A Bank of Russia ordinance opening crypto purchases to ordinary investors takes effect on Nov. 17. Non-qualified buyers will be limited to bitcoin, ether and USDT, up to a yearly amount per intermediary, while qualified investors face no cap. Russian exchanges can start organized crypto trading from Nov. 22 if the central bank signs off on their trading rules.
