title: SEC charges eight Colorado entities over $37 million fake adviser and crypto platform scheme
time: 2026-11-20T09:30:00+09:00
category: US
important: false
---
The SEC on Thursday sued eight Colorado-registered entities it says worked in pairs, one posing as an investment adviser and the other as a crypto trading platform, taking more than $37 million from investors between August 2024 and May 2026. Victims were drawn in through social media ads and chat groups, then shown fake SEC certificates and genuine regulatory filings to build trust. The agency says the people running the scheme are likely based overseas. [Source: Bitcoin Magazine](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/sec-charges-eight-colorado-firms-scam)
