Profits at China's larger industrial firms rose 20.1% from a year earlier in October, up from 14.4% in September, the National Bureau of Statistics said on Friday.

For January through October, profits totalled 6.74 trillion yuan, a 16.0% gain that beat the 15.5% pace through September. Electronics and computer makers roughly doubled their earnings and nonferrous metal smelters added 71.7%, while carmakers saw profits fall 26.0%.
