South Korea's KOSPI finished Friday 204.47 points higher at 4,915.68, a 4.34% gain that took the benchmark back above 4,900.

The tech-heavy KOSDAQ did even better, jumping 53.28 points, or 8.13%, to 708.99 after a morning surge had already triggered a buy-side sidecar that briefly paused program trading.

Brokerages and big chipmakers led the rally in Seoul, which came a day after AI and memory stocks rebounded worldwide.
