important: false
---
Fed Chair Kevin Warsh opened the first hearing of the Fed's Productivity and Jobs Task Force in San Francisco on Monday, laying out three AI paths: broadly shared productivity gains, job losses that lift unemployment, and an AI bust with a sharper jobless rise. He called the staff models exploratory rather than official forecasts and gave no view on rates. The S&P 500 fell about 1.9% on the day.
