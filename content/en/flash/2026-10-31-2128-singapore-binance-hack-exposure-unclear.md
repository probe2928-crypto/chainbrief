TRM Labs said on-chain data from Binance's hot wallets cannot be tied to individual users or countries, leaving Singapore's exposure to the $6.3 billion theft unknown, The Straits Times reported. Binance.com has remained on the Monetary Authority of Singapore's Investor Alert List since 2021, when the regulator ordered it to stop soliciting Singapore residents.
