---
title: Bitcoin slips back to about $60,000 as US spot ETFs log $570 million Monday outflow
time: 2026-11-24T21:27:00+09:00
category: Bitcoin
important: false
---
Bitcoin gave back most of Monday's jump, when Nvidia shares rallied on Jensen Huang's return as CEO and pushed the coin to about $64,400, and traded near $60,150 on Tuesday morning in Europe. U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs posted a net outflow of about $570 million on Monday, the fifth straight day of withdrawals, with BlackRock's IBIT accounting for roughly $509 million, according to SoSoValue data. Fidelity's FBTC was the only fund with a notable inflow, at about $13 million.
