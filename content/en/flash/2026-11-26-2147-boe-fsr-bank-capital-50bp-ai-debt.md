The Bank of England's Financial Policy Committee on Thursday proposed changes that would cut Tier 1 capital requirements for major UK lenders by roughly 50 basis points of risk-weighted assets in normal times, while holding the countercyclical buffer at its 2% neutral setting. The Prudential Regulation Authority will consult on the details before anything takes effect.

In its Financial Stability Report the committee also flagged AI funding, noting that bond sales by the biggest cloud providers are on course to top $305 billion this year, about 8% of global investment-grade issuance. The 10-year gilt yield has climbed about 40 basis points since the July report.
