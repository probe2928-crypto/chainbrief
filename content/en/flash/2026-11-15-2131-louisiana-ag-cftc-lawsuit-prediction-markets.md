title: Louisiana's attorney general hits back after CFTC sues the state over prediction markets
time: 2026-11-15T21:31:00+09:00
category: Regulation
important: false
---
The CFTC filed suit against Louisiana in federal court on Nov. 13, the 12th state it has taken to court over prediction markets. Attorney General Liz Murrill answered on X on Friday, calling CFTC chair Michael Selig an "obsequious standard bearer" for Kalshi. A state restraining order signed Nov. 12 tells 31 companies, including Kalshi, Polymarket, Coinbase and Robinhood, to block Louisiana users, with a hearing set for Nov. 30.
