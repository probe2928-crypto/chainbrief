title: Strategy buys 457 bitcoin and spends $211.8 million buying back STRC preferred shares
time: 2026-11-24T09:27:00+09:00
category: Bitcoin
important: false
---
Strategy said in an 8-K filed Monday that it bought 457 BTC for about $29.6 million between Nov. 16 and Nov. 22, at an average of $64,898, lifting its holdings to 851,305 BTC at an average cost of $75,446. Most of the $176.7 million it raised from selling common stock went elsewhere: together with cash on hand, it paid $211.8 million to repurchase about 2.18 million STRC preferred shares, and the board raised its buyback authorization for those securities to $4 billion from $3 billion.
