category: Bitcoin

important: false
---
BIT Asset Management's Bitcoin Fund ETF started trading on SIX Swiss Exchange on Thursday under the ticker BBIT, holding 350 BTC at launch and charging 0.19% a year.

FINMA approved the fund on Oct. 20 with an exemption from Swiss diversification rules, letting it sit fully in bitcoin held in cold storage at AMINA Bank.

Most bitcoin products in Europe are exchange-traded notes, which are debt of the issuer; BBIT is a fund whose coins sit apart from the manager's balance sheet, and holders can swap bitcoin for shares in kind.
