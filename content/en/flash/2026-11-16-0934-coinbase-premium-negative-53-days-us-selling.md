Bitcoin has traded below its Binance price on Coinbase for 53 straight days through Nov. 15, a sign that US sellers have been more eager than buyers. Binance Research counted 7,438 BTC of aggressive net selling on an unnamed dollar exchange from Nov. 11 to 14, against 2,707 BTC on Binance, and CoinGlass data shows Coinbase spot volume skewed $1.08 billion toward sellers since Oct. 31 while OKX, Bybit and Binance leaned slightly to buyers.
