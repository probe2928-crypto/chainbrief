title: New York judge refuses to halt Kalshi for now; preliminary injunction hearing set for January
time: 2026-11-24T09:30:00+09:00
category: US
important: false
---
New York state Justice Melissa Crane on Monday denied Attorney General Letitia James's request for a temporary restraining order, so Kalshi can keep offering sports event contracts while the case proceeds. Crane raised concerns about 18- to 20-year-olds trading sports contracts in a state where the gambling age is 21, and Kalshi's lawyers said they could look at restricting under-21 users there. Both sides are to report back on safeguards by Nov. 30, with a preliminary injunction hearing planned for January.
