---
title: FinCEN moves to cut MEXC off from U.S. banks over $16 billion in alleged illicit flows
time: 2026-11-08T21:20:00+09:00
category: US
important: true
---
The Treasury's Financial Crimes Enforcement Network issued a final order on Friday under section 9714 of the Combating Russian Money Laundering Act that will bar U.S. financial institutions from moving funds for MEXC Global and its offshore affiliates. FinCEN alleges at least $16 billion in illicit flows through the exchange from May 2021 to May 2026, including Russian sanctions evasion and traffic with Iranian exchangers. The ban takes effect 30 days after publication in the Federal Register; it is not an asset freeze, and MEXC said on X that it is operating normally.
