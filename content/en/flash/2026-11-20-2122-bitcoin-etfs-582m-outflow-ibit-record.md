title: U.S. bitcoin ETFs lose $582 million on Nov. 19 as IBIT posts its biggest one-day outflow
time: 2026-11-20T21:22:00+09:00
category: Bitcoin
important: true
---
Investors pulled a net $582.3 million from U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs on Thursday, the third straight day of outflows, according to Farside data. BlackRock's IBIT accounted for $541.1 million of that, its largest single-day withdrawal on record, while Morgan Stanley's fund took in $49.6 million. Net outflows for November now come to about $3.47 billion, as bitcoin slipped below $62,000.
