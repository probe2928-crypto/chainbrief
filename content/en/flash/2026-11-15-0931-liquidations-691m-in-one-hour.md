category: On-chain

About $691 million of leveraged positions were wiped out across crypto derivatives venues in the hour bitcoin dipped below $64,000, according to CoinGlass data cited by PANews. Longs made up nearly all of it, at roughly $671 million against $19.5 million of shorts.
