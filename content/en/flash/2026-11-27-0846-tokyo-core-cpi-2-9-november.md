Consumer prices in Tokyo's 23 wards excluding fresh food rose 2.9% from a year earlier in November, Japan's statistics bureau said, in line with the consensus and up from 2.6% in October.

Tokyo's figures arrive about three weeks before the nationwide data and are read as an early guide to it, which puts them at the center of the debate over the Bank of Japan's December meeting.

The BOJ policy rate currently stands at 1.25%.
