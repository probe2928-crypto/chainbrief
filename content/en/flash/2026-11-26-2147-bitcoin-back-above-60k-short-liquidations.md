category: Bitcoin

Bitcoin pushed back above $60,000 during the US Thanksgiving holiday, touching $61,530 and gaining about 2.7% over 24 hours after Wednesday's slide toward $57,200 on the tariff proposals and hot inflation data.

CoinGlass data showed roughly $692 million in crypto derivatives liquidated over the day, including about $143 million in bitcoin short positions. US stock markets reopen Friday for a shortened session.
