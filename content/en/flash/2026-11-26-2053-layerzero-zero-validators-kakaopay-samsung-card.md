LayerZero named South Korea's Kakao Pay and Samsung Card, along with ZAN, the Web3 infrastructure arm of Ant Group's Ant Digital Technologies, as genesis validators for Zero, the blockchain it switches on Nov. 30.

Seoul research firm Four Pillars will run Samsung Card's node. Validators take part in consensus and check zero-knowledge proofs rather than re-executing transactions, and voting power follows staked ZRO, split evenly among the first validators until March 31, 2027.
