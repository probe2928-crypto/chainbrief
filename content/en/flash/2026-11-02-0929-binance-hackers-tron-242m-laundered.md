title: Binance hackers move about $242 million through Tron in two days
time: 2026-11-02T09:29:00+09:00
category: On-chain
important: false
---
After Tether froze 378.7 million USDT, the attackers still had about $249 million to work with on Tron, and roughly $242 million of it moved out through Relay, THORChain, Chainflip and other services within two days, according to on-chain analyst SomaXBT. Elliptic estimates about $3.3 billion of the loot sits in assets no issuer can freeze, including 18,270 BTC that has not left the 19 bitcoin addresses named by the FBI.
