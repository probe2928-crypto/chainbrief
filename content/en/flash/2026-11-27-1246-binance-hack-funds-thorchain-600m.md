Chainalysis and Elliptic estimate that more than $600 million of the $6.3 billion taken from Binance on Oct. 29 has been swapped through THORChain, the cross-chain exchange that runs without KYC. Elliptic cautions the figure may count some funds more than once, and tracks a further $342 million through a coinjoin mixer.

TRM Labs puts THORChain's fee take from the Binance-linked swaps at up to $1 million. Co-founder Chad Barraford said in a video on X that 98% of the network's trading is for users' own financial freedom.
