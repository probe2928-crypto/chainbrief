---
title: ARK pulls registrations for two CoinDesk 20 crypto index ETFs
time: 2026-11-22T09:32:00+09:00
category: US
important: false
---
ARK Investment Management asked the SEC on Nov. 20 to withdraw the registration statements for the ARK CoinDesk 20 Crypto ETF and its ex-Bitcoin sibling under Securities Act Rule 477, saying it no longer plans to offer the shares. Both S-1s were filed on Jan. 23, never went effective and sold nothing. ARK's spot bitcoin fund, ARKB, is not affected.
