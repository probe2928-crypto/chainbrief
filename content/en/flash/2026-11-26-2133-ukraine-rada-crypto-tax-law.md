---
title: Ukraine's parliament passes crypto law with 23% tax on gains, effective July 2027
time: 2026-11-26T21:33:00+09:00
category: Regulation
region: eu
important: false
---
Ukraine's parliament on Wednesday adopted bill 10225-d, which legalises the virtual asset market and taxes crypto profits at 23%, made up of 18% personal income tax and a 5% military levy. Assets bought before the law takes effect get a lower combined rate of 10% until the end of 2028.

The law puts the securities commission and the National Bank of Ukraine in charge of supervision and is set to apply from July 1, 2027. It still needs President Volodymyr Zelensky's signature.
