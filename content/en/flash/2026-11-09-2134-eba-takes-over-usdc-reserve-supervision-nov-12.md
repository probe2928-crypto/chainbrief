title: EU banking regulator takes direct charge of USDC reserves on Thursday
time: 2026-11-09T21:34:00+09:00
category: Europe
important: false
---
From Nov. 12, the European Banking Authority becomes the direct supervisor of USDC's reserve assets, own funds, liquidity and recovery and redemption plans, after naming Circle's dollar token the first "significant" e-money token under MiCA last month. France's ACPR, which licensed Circle's EU arm in 2024, keeps oversight of governance, shareholders and fit-and-proper checks. USDC, at roughly $73 billion, is the only one of the 45 e-money tokens notified in the EU to get the label so far.
