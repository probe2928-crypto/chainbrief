category: Exchanges

Coinbase reported third-quarter net income of $719 million, almost all of it from a $715 million gain on crypto it holds, while revenue of about $1.22 billion was flat on the quarter and down 35% from a year earlier. Prediction markets passed $200 million in annualized revenue, and CEO Brian Armstrong said the CFTC has cleared Coinbase to list its own event contracts.
