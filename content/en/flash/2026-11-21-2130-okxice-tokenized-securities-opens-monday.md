OKXICE, the joint venture between OKX and NYSE owner Intercontinental Exchange, plans to open its Tokenized Securities Venue to eligible U.S. customers on Monday, Nov. 23, with more than 70 assets including 59 Direxion ETFs and securities from Circle, Robinhood and Strategy. Trading runs around the clock through liquidity pools on Uniswap v4 on OKX's X Layer network, with Securitize handling tokenization, under the SEC's innovation exemption. OKX says the venue is not a registered exchange or broker-dealer.
