Bitcoin traded near $61,400 on Monday morning in Europe, about 11% above the roughly $55,100 it touched early Saturday, its lowest since September 2024, Unchained reported using CoinGecko and exchange data. CoinDesk tied the bounce to equities: Nasdaq 100 futures were up about 3% and S&P 500 futures close to that after Friday's AI-led selloff, with Nvidia up 8% before the open following a 23% drop last week. Ether was around $1,720. [Source: CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/23/live-updates-bitcoin-climbs-back-above-usd61-000-as-stocks-rebound-from-ai-rout)
