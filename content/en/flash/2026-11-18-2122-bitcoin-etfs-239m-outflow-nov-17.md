U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs posted net outflows of $239.9 million on Tuesday, Nov. 17, with BlackRock's IBIT accounting for $209.5 million of it, according to Farside data. Grayscale's GBTC lost $17.7 million and ARK's ARKB $13.1 million, while spot ether ETFs shed a further $29.1 million. The bitcoin funds have now seen about $1 billion leave over five trading days.
