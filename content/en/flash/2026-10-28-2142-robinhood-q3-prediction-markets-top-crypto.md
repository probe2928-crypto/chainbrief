Robinhood said Oct. 27 that third-quarter revenue rose 12% from a year earlier to a record $1.42 billion. Event contracts brought in $173 million, more than six times last year's level, while crypto revenue dropped 55% to $120 million. Customers traded a record 16.4 billion event contracts in the period.
