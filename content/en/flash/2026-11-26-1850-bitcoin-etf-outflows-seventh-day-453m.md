US spot bitcoin ETFs saw $453.1 million in net outflows on Wednesday, the seventh straight session of withdrawals, with BlackRock's IBIT accounting for most of the money leaving.

Grayscale and Bitwise funds also lost assets, adding to a November that has already set a record for monthly outflows.

US markets are closed Thursday for Thanksgiving and reopen for a shortened session Friday.
