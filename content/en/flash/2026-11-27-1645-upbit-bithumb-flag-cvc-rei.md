South Korea's two largest crypto exchanges, Upbit and Bithumb, flagged Civic (CVC) and REI as trading warning items on Friday and stopped deposits of both tokens from 3:30 p.m. Seoul time.

The exchanges cited thin disclosure on how CVC's token is used and how the project is run, and doubts over whether REI's operator can keep the business going.

A decision to extend the warning, lift it or delist the tokens is due in the week of Dec. 14. The same day, both exchanges extended an existing warning on INIT while they review the team's response.
