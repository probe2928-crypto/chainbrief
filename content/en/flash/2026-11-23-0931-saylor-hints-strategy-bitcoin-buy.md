Strategy executive chairman Michael Saylor shared the company's bitcoin purchase chart on X on Sunday with a caption suggesting the buying isn't over; a similar post on Nov. 8 came a day before Strategy disclosed a new purchase. The company held 850,849 BTC as of Nov. 15 at an average cost of about $75,452, so at Sunday's price near $60,400 crypto.news put the paper loss at roughly $12.8 billion, while Bitcoin Sistemi cited $12.6 billion. crypto.news also noted that MSCI plans to drop Strategy from affected indexes after the Nov. 30 close.
