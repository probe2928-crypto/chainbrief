title: Binance reopens bitcoin and 14 other networks for withdrawals, says 95% of stolen assets replaced
time: 2026-11-01T21:22:00+09:00
category: Exchanges
important: true
---
Binance restarted withdrawals on Sunday in a first phase that began with the bitcoin network shortly after 06:00 UTC, followed by ether, BNB and some USDT and USDC routes, across 15 networks in total. The exchange said it has now bought back about 95% of the roughly $6.3 billion taken in last week's hack, up from 90% a day earlier, with the rest made up of less liquid tokens. No date has been set for the second phase, and deposits stayed open throughout.
