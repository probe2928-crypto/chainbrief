title: ECB accounts show a large number of policymakers could have backed an October hike

A large number of ECB Governing Council members said they could have supported raising rates at the October 28-29 meeting, according to the account of that meeting published Thursday.

The bank held rates in October to gather more evidence, but the account warns inflation is likely to run higher than earlier thought and says second-round effects on wages and prices should be pre-empted even though they have not yet appeared.

Most policymakers have already pointed to a quarter-point hike at the next meeting on December 17.
