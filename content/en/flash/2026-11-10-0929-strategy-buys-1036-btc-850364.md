title: Strategy adds 1,036 bitcoin, lifting holdings to 850,364 BTC
time: 2026-11-10T09:29:00+09:00
category: Bitcoin
important: false
---
Strategy bought 1,036 bitcoin for about $81.9 million between Nov. 2 and Nov. 8, paying an average of $79,012 each, according to a Monday filing with the SEC. The company now holds 850,364 BTC at an average cost of $75,456. It funded the buy by selling about 2.05 million common shares for $283.8 million, and spent $188.3 million of that buying back its STRC preferred stock, ahead of MSCI's index decision expected Nov. 11.
