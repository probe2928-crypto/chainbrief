U.S. spot ether ETFs lost about $1.3 million on Tuesday, Nov. 10, a fourth straight day of net redemptions, according to SoSoValue figures cited by PANews and Bitcoin.com News. That is a small fraction of the roughly $185 million that left the funds on Monday. [Source: Bitcoin.com News](https://news.bitcoin.com/bitcoin-etf/bitcoin-etfs-outflows-fourth-straight-day-2026/)
