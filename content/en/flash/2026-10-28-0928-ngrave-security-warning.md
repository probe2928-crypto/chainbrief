important: false
---
Belgian hardware wallet maker NGRAVE said it is facing a high-level cyber threat and told users to watch for phishing by text, email, fake websites and bogus support staff, adding it never asks for recovery phrases. The warning follows an Oct. 17 incident in which attackers hijacked a third-party push service for its LIQUID app and drained at least $2.56 million from about 70 wallets.
