Binance's 24-hour net outflows have dropped to roughly $3.29 billion, less than half the peak of about $7 billion, according to DeFiLlama data. The exchange plans to restore withdrawals for more networks and assets on Nov. 3 but has not named them or given a start time. The first phase on Nov. 1 covered 15 networks.
