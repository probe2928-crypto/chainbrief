---
title: Fed minutes show almost all officials expect more hikes, a couple floated more than 25 bp
time: 2026-11-19T09:27:00+09:00
category: Macro
important: true
---
Minutes of the Fed's October meeting, released Nov. 18, show nearly every participant saw further rate increases as likely appropriate, even though the committee held the funds rate at 3.75% to 4.00%. A couple of officials said a December move larger than 25 basis points could be needed if the data worsen, and some who backed the hold called it a close call. CME FedWatch put the odds of a quarter-point hike at the Dec. 8-9 meeting near 92%, according to InvestmentNews.
