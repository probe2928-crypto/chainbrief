Bitcoin fell through $64,000 late Saturday UTC, trading near $63,500 on CoinGecko after a 6.6% drop in 24 hours and well under Friday's $67,555 low. Ether did worse, touching $1,746 on Binance, roughly 9.6% lower on the day. Alternative.me's Fear & Greed Index sank to 13, down from 20 on Friday.
