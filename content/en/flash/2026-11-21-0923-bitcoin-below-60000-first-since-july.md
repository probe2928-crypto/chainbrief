---
title: Bitcoin drops below $60,000 for the first time since July
time: 2026-11-21T09:23:00+09:00
category: Bitcoin
important: true
---
Bitcoin slid under $60,000 during Friday's U.S. session, a level it had not seen since July. Reuters put it at $59,381, down about 3.5% on the day, with ether off roughly 5.2% at $1,648.91. CoinGlass data cited by crypto.news showed about $700 million in liquidations across crypto over 24 hours. [Source: Reuters via Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/bitcoin-falls-below-60000-level-5042271)
