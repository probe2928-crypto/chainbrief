Binance's chief financial officer Kaiser Ng is leaving and Justin Yek is taking over the role, according to a statement from co-CEO Yi He obtained by Bloomberg. The exchange said the handover had been planned over the past six months and did not link it to the roughly $6.3 billion theft disclosed in late October. FinanceFeeds reported that Yek was most recently vice president of finance at payments firm Airwallex.
