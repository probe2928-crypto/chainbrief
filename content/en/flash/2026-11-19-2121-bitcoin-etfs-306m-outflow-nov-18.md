---
title: US spot bitcoin ETFs lose $306.5 million on Nov. 18, a second day of outflows
time: 2026-11-19T21:21:00+09:00
category: Bitcoin
important: true
---
None of the 12 US spot bitcoin ETFs took in money on Wednesday, and net outflows reached about $306.5 million, following $239.9 million the day before. BlackRock's IBIT accounted for roughly $148.7 million, nearly half the total, with Fidelity's FBTC next at about $69.3 million. Farside data puts November's net outflows near $2.9 billion so far.
