---
title: SEC staff grants Molecule no-action relief for science "project tokens"
time: 2026-11-25T09:24:00+09:00
category: Regulation
important: false
---
The SEC's Division of Corporation Finance told Molecule AG on Nov. 24 that staff would not recommend enforcement if affiliate LabsCo sells research-funding "Project Tokens" without Securities Act registration, as described in its Nov. 19 request. Staff also won't push for the tokens to be registered as an equity class under Exchange Act Section 12(g). The relief is a staff position tied to the stated facts, and Molecule says its first project will use the model within weeks.
