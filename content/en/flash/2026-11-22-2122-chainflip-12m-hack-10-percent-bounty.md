time: 2026-11-22T21:22:00+09:00
category: On-chain
important: false
---
Cross-chain swap protocol Chainflip now says about $12 million was taken from its vaults on Nov. 21, well above the first estimate of $4.86 million from its Ethereum vault alone. The list includes 122.86 BTC, 520.22 ETH, 3.25 million USDC and 758,141 USDT. Chainflip told the attacker to keep 10% and return the rest by 18:38 UTC on Nov. 23, in which case it would treat the incident as a white-hat recovery and withdraw its complaints to law enforcement; the protocol remains paused and its FLIP token fell about 43% in a day.
