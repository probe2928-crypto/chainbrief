title: EU banking watchdog renews call for rules on crypto firms that route clients into DeFi lending
time: 2026-11-26T09:27:00+09:00
category: Europe
important: false
---
The European Banking Authority used a new report on stablecoins in decentralized finance to repeat its request that the European Commission write rules for crypto firms giving clients access to DeFi lending protocols. MiCA bans interest on e-money tokens, but holders can still earn yield by lending them through DeFi, the EBA noted. Its ideas include suitability checks, added risk warnings, leverage caps and a ban on steering clients toward stablecoins not authorized in the EU.
