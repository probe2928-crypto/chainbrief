title: DOJ gets court approval to seize or restrain over $1.2 billion stolen from Binance
time: 2026-11-13T09:32:00+09:00
category: US
important: true
---
The Justice Department said Thursday it has court authorization to seize or restrain more than $1.2 billion of the crypto stolen from Binance around Oct. 29, a theft it pins on North Korean military hackers the FBI calls TraderTraitor. That sum sits inside a broader freeze of more than $2.7 billion achieved by U.S. agencies and private firms, while the rest is still moving through bridges, mixers and exchanges. The department did not name the court or list the tokens involved, and it also disclosed a separate North Korean deepfake video-call scheme, dubbed FaceStealer, tied to more than $400 million in thefts over 18 months.
