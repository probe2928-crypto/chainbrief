important: false
---
Hong Kong gazetted its Virtual Asset Services Legislation (Amendment) Bill on Friday, bringing virtual asset dealing, advice, management and custody under the anti-money-laundering ordinance. Firms offering those services in the city would need a licence or registration from the Securities and Futures Commission, and banks and stored-value operators supervised by the HKMA would also have to register. The bill goes to the Legislative Council for first reading on Dec. 2. [Source: Odaily](https://www.odaily.news/zh-CN/newsflash/536867)
