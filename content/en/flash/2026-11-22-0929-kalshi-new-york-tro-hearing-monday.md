---
title: Kalshi faces Monday state-court hearing on New York bid to bar it from the state
time: 2026-11-22T09:29:00+09:00
category: US
important: false
---
A New York state judge, Justice Melissa Crane, will hear Attorney General Letitia James's request for a temporary restraining order against Kalshi at 11:30 a.m. ET on Monday, Nov. 23. The order would bar the prediction market from operating within or from New York and from serving users under 21; Kalshi argues it would effectively shut its CFTC-regulated exchange nationwide, which the AG's office disputes. On Friday, U.S. District Judge Lorna Schofield turned down the CFTC's second emergency attempt to halt the state case.
