Bitcoin was changing hands between roughly $60,000 and $61,000 on Sunday, a day after it fell to about $55,300, its lowest level since September 2024. CoinGecko data cited by CEO Economy put Saturday evening's price near $60,121, about 12% lower than a week earlier, while Alternative.me's fear and greed gauge sat at 12, deep in "extreme fear." Leveraged positions worth more than $2.5 billion were wiped out over the 24 hours around the low, most of them bets on higher prices, according to CoinGlass figures cited by Coinpaper.
