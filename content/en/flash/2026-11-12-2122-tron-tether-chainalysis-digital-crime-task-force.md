Tron, Tether and Chainalysis set up a "Digital Crime Task Force" on Thursday to cut the time between spotting illicit USDT flows on Tron and freezing them with law enforcement, The Block and crypto.news reported. Chainalysis takes the seat TRM Labs held in the earlier T3 Financial Crime Unit, which has been dissolved; Tether says it has frozen more than $7 billion tied to illicit activity so far. No new freezes or cases were announced with the launch. [Source: The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-11-12-tron-tether-chainalysis-new-alliance-438514)
