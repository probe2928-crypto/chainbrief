title: Arbitrum DAO vote on handing Binance its frozen hack funds closes Monday
time: 2026-11-16T21:39:00+09:00
category: Exchanges
important: false

Arbitrum token holders are voting on whether to send Binance 8,425.997 ETH and about 74.56 million ARB that were frozen on the network after the exchange's October hack. The funds would go toward covering user losses, and the proposal makes the transfer conditional on full legal indemnification for the DAO. The vote ends Nov. 16.
