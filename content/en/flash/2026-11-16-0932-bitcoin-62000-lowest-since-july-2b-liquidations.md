title: Bitcoin touches about $62,000, its lowest since July, before bouncing to $65,000
time: 2026-11-16T09:32:00+09:00
category: Bitcoin
important: true
---
Bitcoin fell as low as roughly $62,080 on Sunday, Nov. 15, its weakest level since July, before recovering to around $65,000 later in the day. CoinGlass counted close to $2 billion in liquidated derivatives positions over 24 hours, more than 80% of them bets on higher prices. US spot bitcoin ETFs, which shed about $1.9 billion over the seven sessions to Nov. 13, reopen for trading on Monday.
