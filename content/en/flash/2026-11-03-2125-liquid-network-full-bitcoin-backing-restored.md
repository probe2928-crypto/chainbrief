title: Liquid Network says LBTC is fully backed again two months after $320 million exploit
time: 2026-11-03T21:25:00+09:00
category: Bitcoin
important: false
---
The Liquid Federation said on Tuesday that its bitcoin reserves again cover all LBTC one-to-one, closing a gap left by a Sept. 6 exploit that drained about 4,000 BTC. The attacker returned roughly 3,400 BTC a day later; the last 550 BTC arrived in two deposits on Monday, which a Federation board member attributed to Blockstream. The Federation says governance and security changes will follow in the coming weeks.
