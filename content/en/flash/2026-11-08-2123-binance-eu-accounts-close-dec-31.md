---
title: Binance to close some EU accounts on Dec. 31, with idle-balance fees from Nov. 29
time: 2026-11-08T21:23:00+09:00
category: Europe
important: false
---
Binance is telling users in Spain, France and Poland, and according to posts on X also Italy, that their accounts will close on Dec. 31 and that a monthly fee of 0.025% of remaining crypto, or 10 USDC if higher, starts on Nov. 29. Deposits are blocked but withdrawals remain open. The exchange has no license under the EU's MiCA rules, whose transition period ended this year, and France's AMF lists Binance France's old registration as lapsed since July 2. [Source: CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/11/07/binance-users-in-some-european-countries-told-to-pull-crypto-before-fees-kick-in-with-accounts-closing-by-year-end)
