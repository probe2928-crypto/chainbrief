XRP dropped as low as 89 cents, its weakest level since November 2024 and about 36% lower for the quarter. U.S.-listed XRP funds posted $19.13 million of net outflows in the week to Nov. 13, their largest weekly withdrawal since March, according to SoSoValue data. The funds have taken in about $1.5 billion since launching a year ago.
