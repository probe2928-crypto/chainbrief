title: U.S. producer prices up 6.5% from a year ago in October, fastest pace since 2022
time: 2026-11-14T21:31:00+09:00
category: Macro
important: false
---
The U.S. producer price index rose 0.3% in October from September, a little below the 0.4% forecast, but the year-on-year rate reached 6.5%, the fastest since November 2022, with core prices up 4.9%. CryptoTicker tied Friday afternoon's leg lower in bitcoin to the report, which adds pressure for interest rates to stay high.
