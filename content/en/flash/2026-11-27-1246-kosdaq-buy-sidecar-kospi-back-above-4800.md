South Korea's KOSDAQ surged more than 5% on Friday morning, tripping a buy-side "sidecar" at 10:22 a.m. that froze program buy orders for five minutes.

The KOSPI, which dipped to 4,682 early on, turned around and stood near 4,815 by about 11:20 a.m., up roughly 2.2% and back above 4,800. Hopes that authorities will enlarge the stock market stabilization fund lifted sentiment, with foreign and institutional investors buying robotics, biotech and battery names.
