The CFTC's innovation task force will host its first Frontier Forum on Wednesday, focused on how artificial intelligence could reshape U.S. derivatives markets and their oversight. Participants include crypto firms Chainlink, Hedera and Tempo, trading-technology companies such as OneChronos, and the Investment Company Institute. Topics include AI agents that use blockchains and stablecoins to trade without human input, and whether current rules cover them. [Source: Bloomingbit](https://en.bloomingbit.io/feed/news/122987)
