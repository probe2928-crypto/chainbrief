U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs posted about $643 million in net outflows on Oct. 30, ending a four-day run of inflows and marking the largest one-day withdrawal since June. BlackRock's IBIT lost roughly $234 million and Fidelity's FBTC about $201 million, while ether funds shed another $156 million or so, according to Farside data.
