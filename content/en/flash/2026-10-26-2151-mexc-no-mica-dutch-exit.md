---
title: MEXC confirms it lacks MiCA authorization as Dutch offboarding nears its end
time: 2026-10-26T21:51:00+09:00
category: Europe
important: false
---
Exchange MEXC says in an updated country restrictions guide that it is not authorized to provide crypto-asset services under the EU's MiCA rules, as it finishes closing accounts for users in the Netherlands. The guide gives Nov. 16 as the final offboarding date, later than the Oct. 31 completion date in an earlier notice, and MEXC has also said it will stop serving users in Russia and Belarus.
