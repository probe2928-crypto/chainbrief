U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs posted $398.6 million in net outflows on Monday, adding to the $643 million that left on Friday. BlackRock's IBIT alone shed $405.3 million, more than the group's net total, while Fidelity's FBTC took in about $6.7 million. Bitcoin slipped toward $76,500 on Tuesday as investors waited on U.S. midterm results. [Source: crypto.news](https://crypto.news/why-is-bitcoin-price-down-today-btc-76000-support/)
