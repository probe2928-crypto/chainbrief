---
Deutsche Bank began holding bitcoin, ether and three stablecoins (USDC, EURC and AllUnity's EURAU) for institutional and corporate clients on Wednesday, with the bank keeping the private keys itself, Cointelegraph and crypto.news reported. The service runs under a MiCA custody and transfer licence that BaFin granted on Oct. 12 and uses technology from Taurus; trading and tokenized securities are not part of the first offering. The bank also signed a memorandum with its asset manager on digital-asset products, without naming any.
