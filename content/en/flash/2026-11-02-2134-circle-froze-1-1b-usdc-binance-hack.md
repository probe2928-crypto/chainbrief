time: 2026-11-02T21:34:00+09:00
category: US
important: false
---
Circle said it has frozen more than $1.1 billion in USDC connected to last week's Binance theft, up from the roughly $823 million it locked in the attacker's wallet within minutes of the hack. The company has not explained the gap between the two figures. Bitcoin and ether, which no issuer can freeze, make up an estimated $2.4 billion of the stolen funds, according to TRM Labs. [Source: American Banker](https://www.americanbanker.com/news/6b-binance-heist-provokes-stablecoin-freeze-dilemma)
