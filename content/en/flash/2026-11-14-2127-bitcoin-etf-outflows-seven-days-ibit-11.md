U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs had net outflows of $185.4 million on Friday, the seventh trading day in a row of withdrawals, according to Farside Investors data. BlackRock's IBIT accounted for $143.5 million and Fidelity's FBTC for $41.9 million, which brought the week's total to about $1.07 billion and the two-week tally to roughly $2.35 billion. IBIT alone has now seen 11 straight sessions of outflows, about $908 million of them this week.
