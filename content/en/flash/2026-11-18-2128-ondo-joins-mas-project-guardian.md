important: false

Ondo Finance said it has joined Project Guardian, the Monetary Authority of Singapore's industry programme for testing tokenised assets. The U.S. tokenisation firm said it will share its experience distributing tokenised products across platforms and borders, with a focus on investor access, investor protection and interoperability between jurisdictions. The announcement came during the Singapore FinTech Festival week.
