U.S. spot ether ETFs had about $184.6 million in net redemptions on Monday, Nov. 9, the third trading day in a row of outflows, according to SoSoValue data. The funds still hold about $12.07 billion in combined net assets, while ether traded near $2,120 on Tuesday.
