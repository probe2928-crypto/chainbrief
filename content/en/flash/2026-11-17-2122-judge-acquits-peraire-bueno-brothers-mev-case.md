title: Judge acquits Peraire-Bueno brothers in $25 million Ethereum MEV case
time: 2026-11-17T21:22:00+09:00
category: US
important: false
---
U.S. District Judge Jessica Clarke in Manhattan on Monday granted Anton and James Peraire-Bueno's motion for acquittal on wire fraud, wire fraud conspiracy and money laundering conspiracy charges, ending the case without a retrial. Prosecutors had said the brothers used a flaw in MEV-boost software in April 2023 to drain about $25 million from trading bots in roughly 12 seconds; the judge found no material misrepresentation, which wire fraud requires. Their first trial ended in a mistrial in November 2025.
