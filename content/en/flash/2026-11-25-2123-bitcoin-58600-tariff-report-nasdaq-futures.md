Bitcoin traded near $58,650 at 11:50 UTC on Wednesday, down about 2.6% in 24 hours, after reports that Washington could announce new tariffs on 16 trading partners, including China and the EU, sent Nasdaq 100 futures down roughly 2.4%. Ether fell to about $1,640 and the total crypto market dropped to around $2.01 trillion, while the VIX volatility index climbed back above 30. Traders are waiting for October PCE inflation data at 13:30 UTC, with U.S. markets shut on Thursday for Thanksgiving.
