Bitcoin miner turned data center developer Cipher Digital reported third-quarter revenue of $26.7 million, down from $71.7 million a year earlier and about $11 million short of analyst estimates. Mining brought in $20.8 million and its first high-performance computing lease added $5.9 million; a $127.2 million warrant revaluation turned the quarter into a $31.6 million net profit. The company also extended its Barber Lake lease with an AI lab to 20 years, lifting contracted revenue at the site to about $9.1 billion.
