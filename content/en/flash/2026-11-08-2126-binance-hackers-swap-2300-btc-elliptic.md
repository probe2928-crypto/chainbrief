title: Binance attackers turn about $175 million of altcoins into 2,300 bitcoin, Elliptic says
time: 2026-11-08T21:26:00+09:00
category: On-chain
important: false
---
Blockchain analytics firm Elliptic says the group behind the Oct. 29 Binance hack has converted roughly $175 million of stolen SOL, XRP, TRX and other tokens into about 2,300 BTC, using swaps, cross-chain bridges such as THORChain and coinjoin mixers. The laundering has spread across more than 20,000 wallets on 16 blockchains, and Arkham data showed attacker-linked bitcoin at about 18,385 BTC on Saturday. Separately, Binance said on Nov. 8 that withdrawals for HYPE and APT have been reopened.
