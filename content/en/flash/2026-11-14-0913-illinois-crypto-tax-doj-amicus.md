---
title: Justice Department, Wall Street groups and DeFi firms line up against Illinois' 0.2% crypto tax
time: 2026-11-14T09:13:00+09:00
category: US
important: false
---
Illinois' Digital Asset Tax Act would charge 0.2% of the full value each time a resident exchanges, transfers or stores crypto through a broker, even when nothing is sold; the Crypto Council for Innovation and the Blockchain Association are challenging it in Sangamon County Circuit Court, and the state has paused the tax until July 1, 2027. The Justice Department filed a statement of interest on Nov. 6, the same day the U.S. Chamber of Commerce and SIFMA filed a friend-of-the-court brief. A coalition led by the DeFi Education Fund, a16z, Paradigm and Uniswap followed on Nov. 11, arguing the state's draft rules would reach software developers who never hold customer funds.
