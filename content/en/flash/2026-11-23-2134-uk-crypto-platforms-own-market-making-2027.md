---
title: UK sets path for outside market makers as platforms are barred from trading on their own venues
time: 2026-11-23T21:34:00+09:00
category: Europe
important: false
---
The UK Treasury made the Cryptoassets (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations 2026 on Nov. 18, and crypto.news and Blockonomi report the instrument lets independent firms make markets on UK crypto trading platforms without a separate dealing permission. The other half of the rule is already in the FCA's June policy statement: from Oct. 25, 2027, a platform operator may not deal on its own account on the venue it runs, apart from matched-principal trades, and must disclose its incentive deals with market makers. Firms have until Feb. 28, 2027 to apply for authorisation.
