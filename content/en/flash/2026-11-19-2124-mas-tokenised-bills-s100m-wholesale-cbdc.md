The Monetary Authority of Singapore said on Thursday it issued S$100 million of four-week tokenised MAS Bills on Nov. 6, settled in wholesale central bank digital currency on its Singapore Dollar Test Network. Five primary dealers took part, and bids came in at more than twice the amount on offer. MAS plans to scale up issuance, add more institutions and use the bills for collateral management, with live trials in 2027.
