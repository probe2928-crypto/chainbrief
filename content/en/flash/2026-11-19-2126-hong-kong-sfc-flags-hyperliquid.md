important: false
---
Hong Kong's Securities and Futures Commission put "Hyperliquid/Hyperliquid Hong Kong" on its list of suspicious virtual asset trading platforms on Nov. 18. The regulator said the platform is not licensed in Hong Kong and is suspected of running a trading venue there or actively marketing to the local public. Hyperliquid's HYPE token dipped to about $65.50 that evening and was back near $68.80 by midday Thursday.
