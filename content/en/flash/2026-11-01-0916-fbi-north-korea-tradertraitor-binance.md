---
title: FBI blames North Korea's TraderTraitor for Binance theft
time: 2026-11-01T09:16:00+09:00
category: US
important: true
---
The FBI on Saturday attributed the theft of more than $6 billion from Binance to TraderTraitor, the North Korean group it also tied to last year's $1.5 billion Bybit hack. The bureau said most of the stolen assets have been swapped into ether and asked exchanges, analytics firms and node operators to block transactions linked to addresses it published.
