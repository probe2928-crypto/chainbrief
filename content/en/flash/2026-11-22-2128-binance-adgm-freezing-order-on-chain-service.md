title: Binance's $6.5 billion Abu Dhabi freezing order is being served to hacker wallets on-chain
time: 2026-11-22T21:28:00+09:00
category: Exchanges
important: false
---
Binance holds a worldwide freezing order from Abu Dhabi Global Market's courts covering up to $6.5 billion linked to the Oct. 29 theft from the exchange, with notices served directly on-chain to the wallets holding stolen funds. The cap is a ceiling, not a recovery: Binance counts about $2.85 billion already frozen or intercepted, mostly stablecoins, while blockchain analytics firm Elliptic tracked roughly 488,000 ETH moved out of the attackers' largest wallets between Nov. 17 and 20. The court is due to sit again on Dec. 1.
