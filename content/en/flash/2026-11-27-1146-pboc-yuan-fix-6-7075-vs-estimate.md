The People's Bank of China fixed the dollar-yuan reference rate at 6.7075 on Friday, a touch stronger for the yuan than Thursday's 6.7096.

The gap with the market estimate of 6.6604 is the bigger story: at 471 pips on the weak side, the fix keeps a firm lid on how far the onshore yuan, which can trade 2% either side of the midpoint, is allowed to climb.
