Rep. Maxine Waters, the top Democrat on the House Financial Services Committee, asked colleagues in a Nov. 6 letter to back her as chair once Democrats take the House in January. She wrote that she would stop what she called the Trump family's crypto corruption and set rules for legitimate crypto firms that are tough but fair. Rep. Sean Casten of Illinois entered the race for the same post a day earlier.
