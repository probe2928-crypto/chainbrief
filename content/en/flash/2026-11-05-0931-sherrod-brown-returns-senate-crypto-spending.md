important: false

Former Senate Banking Committee chair Sherrod Brown beat Republican Sen. Jon Husted in Ohio, two years after a crypto-funded campaign helped push him out. Politico put industry spending on this year's race above $55 million, while Reuters counted more than $68 million before the vote. With Democrats now holding the Senate, Elizabeth Warren rather than Brown is expected to lead the Banking Committee.
