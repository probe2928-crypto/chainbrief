Barclays held about 3.94 million shares of BlackRock's IBIT worth roughly $187 million on Sept. 30, up 48% in share count from June, according to 13F filings reported by crypto.news and Cointelegraph; IBIT's own price rose about 41% in the quarter, so much of the dollar jump is market gains. Citigroup disclosed a new $119 million stake in Fidelity's FBTC and raised its IBIT holding to about 521,000 shares. The snapshots predate November's run of ETF outflows, and the Q3 filing deadline is Nov. 16. [Source: crypto.news](https://crypto.news/barclays-citi-bitcoin-etf-holdings-q3/)
