---
title: MAS turns its tokenisation testnet into SG Digital Ledger and publishes collateral framework
time: 2026-11-20T09:33:00+09:00
category: Singapore
important: false
---
The Monetary Authority of Singapore said on Thursday its Singapore Dollar Test Network, used this month to issue S$100 million of tokenised MAS Bills, will be renamed SG Digital Ledger and run as an operational utility, with take-up left to market demand. It also published an industry framework for using tokens as collateral, covering eligibility, custody, valuation, return and enforcement, with contributors including DBS, ISDA, SGX Group and UBS.
