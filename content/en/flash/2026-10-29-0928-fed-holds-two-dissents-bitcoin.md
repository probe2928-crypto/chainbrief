The Federal Reserve kept its target range at 3.75% to 4.00% on Oct. 28 in a 10-2 vote, with Governor Lisa Cook and Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack dissenting in favor of another quarter-point increase. The decision follows September's hike and a rise in consumer inflation to 3.7%. Bitcoin barely moved on the news and held near $81,000.
