---
title: Coinbase converts 14 non-MiCA stablecoins for EEA retail users, including USDT
time: 2026-10-31T09:28:00+09:00
category: Europe
important: false
---
Coinbase carried out a one-time conversion on Oct. 30 of 14 stablecoins that lack MiCA authorization, among them USDT, PYUSD, USD1 and DAI, swapping remaining EEA retail balances into USDC or another supported asset at market rates. Tokens sent to Coinbase after the cutoff will not be credited; the exchange noted that MiCA's transition period for issuers ended July 1 without extension. [Source: Unchained](https://unchainedcrypto.com/coinbases-one-time-mica-stablecoin-conversion-runs-friday-for-eea-users/)
