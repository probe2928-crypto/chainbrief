Drones of Iranian origin hit Kuwaiti military installations on Thursday, killing two Italian technicians who were working at an air-defence detection station.

Italy's Foreign Minister Antonio Tajani told the Senate the two men worked for Rheinmetall Italia. Kuwait's army said earlier it had intercepted several hostile drones aimed at a "vital site" and that four people were wounded.

The deaths add to a run of strikes on Gulf energy sites and tankers near the Strait of Hormuz, three days before OPEC+ meets on Nov. 29 to set 2027 output policy.
