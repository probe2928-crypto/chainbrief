---
title: FinCEN and Fed drop plan to cut cross-border transfer threshold to $250, including crypto
time: 2026-11-09T21:28:00+09:00
category: Regulation
important: false
---
A 2020 proposal that would have lowered the Bank Secrecy Act recordkeeping and "travel rule" threshold for transfers starting or ending outside the U.S. from $3,000 to $250, crypto transfers included, is formally dead. FinCEN and the Federal Reserve Board filed the withdrawal on Nov. 6, and it takes effect on publication in the Federal Register today, Nov. 9, so the $3,000 line stays. The agencies pointed to the five years since the proposal, which drew nearly 3,000 comments, and to changes in the industry since then, including the GENIUS Act.
