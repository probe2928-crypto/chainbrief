U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs posted net outflows of $95 million on Tuesday, Nov. 10, their fourth losing session in a row, according to SoSoValue data reported by PANews and Bitcoin.com News. BlackRock's IBIT accounted for $78.2 million of the redemptions, extending its streak to eight sessions, while Morgan Stanley's MSBT took in about $20 million. Total assets in the funds stand at about $92.8 billion. [Source: Bitcoin.com News](https://news.bitcoin.com/bitcoin-etf/bitcoin-etfs-outflows-fourth-straight-day-2026/)
