New Zealand's Electoral Commission released final results from the Nov. 7 election, leaving Prime Minister Christopher Luxon's National-ACT-NZ First coalition with 65 of 130 seats, one short of a majority.

National lost two seats once about 400,000 special votes were counted, falling to 40. That hands the balance of power to the centrist Opportunity party and its seven MPs; Luxon has warned talks could take time, and a fresh election is possible if no deal is reached.
