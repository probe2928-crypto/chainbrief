title: Euronext suspends CoinShares' Zcash ETP in Paris on its first day
time: 2026-10-29T21:44:00+09:00
category: Europe
important: false
---
Euronext suspended trading in the CoinShares Zcash ETP (ZCSH) in Paris on Oct. 28, citing settlement requirements that the central securities depository cannot yet fully support. The product would have been Europe's fourth Zcash ETP after earlier listings by 21Shares, Virtune and Valor.
