---
title: About 13 EH/s of bitcoin mining power was hijacked overnight, but no block was found
time: 2026-11-15T21:34:00+09:00
category: On-chain
important: false
---
Roughly 64,000 mining machines, about 13 exahashes per second of computing power, were redirected to the OCEAN pool with payouts set to an attacker's address starting around 8 p.m. U.S. Central time on Nov. 14. CoreX Hosting raised the alarm and later said the attack appeared to be over. Because the hijacked machines did not mine a block in that time, the attacker earned nothing.
