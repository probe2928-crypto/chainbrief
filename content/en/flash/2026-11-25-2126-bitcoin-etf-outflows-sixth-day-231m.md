U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs saw net outflows of about $231.6 million on Tuesday, Nov. 24, according to SoSoValue data, the sixth trading day in a row of withdrawals. BlackRock's IBIT accounted for about $175.3 million of the total, and spot ether ETFs lost a further $64 million. The figure is well under Monday's roughly $570 million exit.
