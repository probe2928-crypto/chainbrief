title: Louisiana sues Kalshi, Polymarket, Robinhood, Coinbase and six other prediction market platforms
time: 2026-11-13T21:23:00+09:00
category: US
important: false
---
Louisiana Attorney General Liz Murrill filed suit on Thursday against ten prediction market operators, including Kalshi, Polymarket, Robinhood, Coinbase, Crypto.com and Gemini, and is asking a court for a restraining order and an injunction against their event contracts. Kalshi responded with its own emergency request for a restraining order, and Robinhood said its contracts fall under CFTC oversight through its derivatives unit. The Block counts Louisiana as the 11th state to sue prediction markets; Covers puts the tally at 13.
