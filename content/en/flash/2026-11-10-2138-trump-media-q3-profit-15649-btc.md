Trump Media & Technology Group reported third-quarter net income of $409.7 million, against a $54.8 million loss a year earlier, with gains on digital assets doing almost all the work while revenue came to about $3 million. Its bitcoin holdings rose by a net 1,510 coins in the quarter to roughly 15,649 BTC, according to its quarterly filing.
