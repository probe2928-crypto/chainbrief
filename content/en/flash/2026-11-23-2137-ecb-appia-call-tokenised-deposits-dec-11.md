title: ECB invites banks and firms into Appia tokenisation tests; first applications due Dec. 11
time: 2026-11-23T21:37:00+09:00
category: Europe
important: false
---
The Eurosystem is asking market participants to join the next phase of Appia, its project for a tokenised European financial system, in three streams: tokenised deposits and payments, tokenised securities, and governance of a shared network. Firms must apply as groups rather than alone, and applications for the first experiments close at noon CET on Dec. 11, with later ones assessed on a rolling basis. CoinPaprika reports the tests would settle in a mock version of tokenised central bank money, not a live system.
