Hong Kong's Securities and Futures Commission on Monday approved the first tokenized funds offered to the public for secondary trading on a licensed virtual asset platform. Two ChinaAMC (Hong Kong) money market funds, one in Hong Kong dollars and one in U.S. dollars, can now be bought and sold around the clock on Futu-owned Cheetah Trade, with primary subscriptions and redemptions as a backstop. The SFC said it will monitor trading closely before considering a wider range of products. [Source: RTHK](https://news.rthk.hk/rthk/ch/component/k2/1875342-20261026.htm)
