---
title: Utah moves to drop 14 criminal charges against Kalshi after it leaves the state
time: 2026-11-25T21:20:00+09:00
category: US
important: false
---
Utah Attorney General Derek Brown's office filed on Monday to dismiss all 14 criminal charges against Kalshi without prejudice, which lets prosecutors refile them if the prediction market comes back. Kalshi had suspended its Utah operations the same day, and the state's separate civil case against the company continues. Kalshi's general counsel said disagreements over jurisdiction should not be treated as crimes.
