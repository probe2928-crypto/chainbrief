title: HashKey On-Chain adopts Coinbase's Base as its core network
time: 2026-11-19T09:30:00+09:00
category: Exchanges
important: false
---
Hong Kong's HashKey Group said on Nov. 18 that its HashKey On-Chain business will use Base, the blockchain built by Coinbase, as its core network, while its own HSK Chain keeps running separately. The two sides plan permissioned lending for eligible clients through Morpho and will make Base and HSK Chain the default networks for HashKey's tokenised assets. No amounts or launch dates were given, and terms await formal documentation.
