---
title: CFTC's final prediction-market rule goes to White House review
time: 2026-11-24T21:30:00+09:00
category: Regulation
important: false
---
The White House Office of Information and Regulatory Affairs logged the CFTC's final rule "Prediction Markets; Public Interest Determinations" as received on Nov. 20, with the review listed as pending. The rule, proposed in June, sets out when event contracts tied to gaming, war, terrorism, assassination or unlawful activity can be barred as contrary to the public interest; the final text has not been published and could differ from the proposal. No timeline for the review has been disclosed.
