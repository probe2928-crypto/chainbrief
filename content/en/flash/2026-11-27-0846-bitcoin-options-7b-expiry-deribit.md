Bitcoin options with a notional value of roughly $7 billion are set to expire on Deribit at 08:00 UTC on Friday, alongside about $600 million of ether options.

Together the two contracts account for around a quarter of open interest on the exchange, and settlement is based on the average index price over the final 30 minutes.

The expiry lands in a thin post-Thanksgiving session, with bitcoin hovering near $60,000.
