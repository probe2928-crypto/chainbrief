---
title: Binance stablecoin reserves down about $9 billion since hack, CryptoQuant data show
time: 2026-11-09T09:22:00+09:00
category: Exchanges
important: false
---
Ethereum-based stablecoins held on Binance fell from roughly $41 billion to about $32 billion around the Oct. 29 hack, according to CryptoQuant figures, while its bitcoin reserve slid by about 56,000 BTC to some 673,700 and its ether reserve by about 400,000 ETH. Binance reopened HYPE and APT withdrawals on Nov. 8, ahead of its own end-of-next-week target; other withdrawal routes are still under security review.
