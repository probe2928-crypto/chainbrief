Payward reported third-quarter total platform margin, its new headline revenue measure, of $487 million on Thursday, about 12% below a year earlier but up from $436 million in the second quarter. Adjusted EBITDA came to $60 million, up from $23 million in Q2 yet roughly two-thirds lower than a year ago. Platform trading volume was $353 billion, 22% higher than the prior quarter and 30% lower year over year. [Source: Cryptonews](https://cryptonews.net/news/finance/33750072/)
