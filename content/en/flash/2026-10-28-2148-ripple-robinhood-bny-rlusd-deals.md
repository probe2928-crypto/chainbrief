title: Ripple signs RLUSD deals with Robinhood and BNY a day after its bank charter win
time: 2026-10-28T21:48:00+09:00
category: US
important: false
---
Ripple used its Swell event on Oct. 27 to announce that Robinhood will bring the RLUSD stablecoin to its retail app later this year, use it on the XRP Ledger for institutional settlement and adopt Ripple Treasury for cash management. BNY's digital asset custody platform now holds XRP and RLUSD, and the two plan to let bank clients mint and redeem the token directly. RLUSD's market value is about $2.57 billion.
