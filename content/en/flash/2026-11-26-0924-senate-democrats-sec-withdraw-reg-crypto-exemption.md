---
title: Senate Democrats ask SEC to scrap Regulation Crypto Assets and the tokenized-stock exemption
time: 2026-11-26T09:24:00+09:00
category: US
important: false
---
Sens. Elizabeth Warren, Chris Van Hollen and Tina Smith led two letters to SEC Chair Paul Atkins on Nov. 25, one asking the agency to withdraw its August proposal letting crypto projects raise up to $5 million over four years, or $75 million within a year, without Securities Act registration. The second, also signed by Sen. Jack Reed, asks the SEC to rescind its five-year innovation exemption that lets tokenized securities venues such as OKXICE operate without registering as exchanges. The SEC did not immediately comment.
