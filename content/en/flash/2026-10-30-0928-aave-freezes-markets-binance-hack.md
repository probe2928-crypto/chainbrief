title: Aave freezes all DAO-run V3 and V4 markets after Binance hacker borrows ETH
time: 2026-10-30T09:28:00+09:00
category: On-chain
important: false
---
Aave paused new deposits and borrowing across every DAO-operated V3 and V4 market, while still letting users withdraw and repay, after a wallet linked to the Binance attack posted stolen USDT as collateral and borrowed roughly 20,000 ETH. Aave said its contracts were not affected and positions remain overcollateralized.
