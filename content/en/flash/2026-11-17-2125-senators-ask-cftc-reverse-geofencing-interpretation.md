---
title: Nine Senate Democrats ask CFTC to withdraw prediction-market geofencing order
time: 2026-11-17T21:25:00+09:00
category: Regulation
important: false
---
Nine Democratic senators led by Adam Schiff wrote to CFTC Chairman Michael Selig on Nov. 16 asking him to withdraw the agency's Nov. 5 interpretation telling registered exchanges to stop geofencing certain event contracts, or to freeze it until court cases are resolved. The senators, from California, Nevada, Washington, Michigan and Minnesota, say the move clashes with court orders and state and tribal law. They also asked the CFTC to preserve related records dating back to Jan. 1, 2025, and to confirm by Nov. 23 that it is doing so.
