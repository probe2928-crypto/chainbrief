Michael Saylor shared Strategy's bitcoin accumulation chart on X on Sunday, the kind of post that has usually come a day before a Monday purchase filing, though it gave no new figure. Strategy held 849,327 BTC as of Nov. 1 at an average cost of about $75,451, close to where bitcoin traded over the weekend. MSCI is expected on Nov. 11 to name the companies caught by its new eligibility rules, with Strategy and Metaplanet at risk of removal effective after the Nov. 30 close.
