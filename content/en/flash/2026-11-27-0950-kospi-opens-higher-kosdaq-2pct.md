South Korea's KOSPI opened Friday up 17.59 points, or 0.37%, at 4,728.80, while the tech-heavy KOSDAQ started 1.34% higher at 664.52 and stretched its gain past 2% within the first 15 minutes, trading above 670.

The move came a day after the Bank of Korea raised its base rate a quarter point to 3.25%, and with no overnight lead from Wall Street, which was shut for Thanksgiving.
