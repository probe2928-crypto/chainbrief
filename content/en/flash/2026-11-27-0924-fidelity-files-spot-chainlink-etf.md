Fidelity filed an S-1 registration with the SEC on Wednesday for the Fidelity Chainlink Fund, which would hold LINK directly and list on Nasdaq under the ticker FLNK. The filing says the trust will not stake its tokens, and names Anchorage Digital Bank, BitGo Bank & Trust and Coinbase Custody as custodians.

No fee or launch date is given. Grayscale and Bitwise already run spot LINK funds, with about $112 million and $42 million in assets respectively as of Nov. 24.
