Japan's Nikkei 225 rose 1,211.95 points, or 2.11%, to finish Friday at 58,711.11, its second straight gain.

Chip-material makers and other AI-linked names led the bounce after weeks of selling driven by doubts over the AI trade, with rising stocks outnumbering decliners about two to one on the Tokyo Stock Exchange.

Wall Street reopens after Thanksgiving for a shortened session that ends at 1 p.m. New York time.
