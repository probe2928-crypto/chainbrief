category: Exchanges

---
Binance's SAFU emergency fund now holds about 7,500 BTC, roughly half of the 15,000 BTC it held after converting from stablecoins in February, after four withdrawals on Oct. 31 to help cover the $6.3 billion theft. At around $77,000 per coin, the remainder is worth about $580 million. Binance says it will rebuild the fund to about $1 billion but has given no timetable.
