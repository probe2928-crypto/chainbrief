time: 2026-11-21T21:27:00+09:00
category: US
important: false
---
U.S. spot bitcoin funds shed another $124.5 million on Friday, Nov. 20, led by $72.4 million out of Fidelity's FBTC, according to Farside data. That left the group down about $1.25 billion for the week and $3.59 billion for November; ether funds lost a further $221.7 million over the week. Thursday's $541.1 million exit from BlackRock's IBIT, its largest since launch, accounted for most of the weekly total.
