---
title: Waller says Fed will supply core settlement as tokenized money spreads
time: 2026-11-18T09:29:00+09:00
category: US
important: false
---
Fed Governor Christopher Waller told The Clearing House's annual conference in New York on Nov. 17 that stablecoins, tokenized deposits and tokenized money market funds can coexist, with the Fed acting as core settlement provider and industry convener. He said systemically important payments may need more conservative, permissioned designs, and that atomic settlement will not replace netting everywhere. He also pointed to the Binance breach as an example of risks in programmable blockchains.
