Germany's GfK consumer climate rose just 0.7 points to -30.2 heading into December, according to the NIM institute, short of the -29.0 economists expected; November was revised to -30.9.

Income expectations improved to -13.9, but economic expectations slid 3.7 points to -1.6, ending a six-month run of gains, while price expectations jumped to 2.8 from 1.0.

The readings point to a muted Christmas season for Europe's largest economy.
