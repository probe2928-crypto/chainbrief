title: Binance says hack losses are fully covered, posts a 198-token reserve snapshot

category: Exchanges
important: false
---
Binance said on Nov. 12 it has finished covering the roughly $6.34 billion in customer assets hit by its Oct. 29 breach, paying from part of its SAFU fund, surplus futures insurance money and company cash, and buying back the affected tokens on the market and over the counter. A new proof-of-reserves snapshot taken at 00:00 UTC on Nov. 12 covers 198 tokens, up from 44, and the zero-knowledge proof users need to check 1:1 backing will take about 40 to 50 hours to generate. Covering the losses is not the same as recovering the stolen funds, and some withdrawals are still being reopened in stages.
