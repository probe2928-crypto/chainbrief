Strategy is not the only treasury-style company leaving MSCI benchmarks on Nov. 30: Metaplanet goes from the Japan Small Cap index, solana holder Upexi from the USA Micro Cap index and uranium holder Yellow Cake from the UK Small Cap index, Bitcoin Magazine and Unchained reported. Ether treasury firm SharpLink stays in but was placed on the watchlist, alongside names such as TON Strategy. Tallies of MSCI's final list count six deletions and 19 watchlist entries.
