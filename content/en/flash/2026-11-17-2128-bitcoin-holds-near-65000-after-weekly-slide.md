Bitcoin traded around $64,900 to $65,000 on Tuesday, little changed over 24 hours after closing Monday at about $64,810 in a range of $63,840 to $65,634. The coin is still down roughly 13% over seven days, with ether off a similar amount near $1,830. Spot bitcoin ETFs logged seven straight days of net outflows through Nov. 13, about $1.9 billion in total.
