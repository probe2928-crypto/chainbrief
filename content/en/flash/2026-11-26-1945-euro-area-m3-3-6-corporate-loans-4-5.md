title: Euro area M3 growth holds at 3.6% in October as business lending picks up to 4.5%

category: Europe

Broad money in the euro area grew 3.6% from a year earlier in October, matching an upwardly revised September reading, ECB data released Thursday showed.

Adjusted loans to non-financial companies accelerated to 4.5% from 4.3%, while household lending stayed at 3.1%; narrow money M1 slowed to 2.4% from 2.7%.

The ECB publishes the account of its latest policy meeting later on Thursday, its first window into how the last decision was argued.
