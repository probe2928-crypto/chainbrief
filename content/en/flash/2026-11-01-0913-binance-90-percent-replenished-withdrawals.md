title: Binance says 90% of stolen assets replaced, keeps Nov. 1 withdrawal target
time: 2026-11-01T09:13:00+09:00
category: Exchanges
important: true
---
Binance said Saturday it has replaced 90% of the roughly $6.3 billion taken from its hot wallet, up from about 80% a day earlier, with its ten most-held assets, including BTC, ETH, USDT and USDC, now fully covered. The exchange still plans to reopen withdrawals token by token on Sunday, Nov. 1, while a full zero-knowledge proof of reserves is expected around Nov. 3 UTC.
