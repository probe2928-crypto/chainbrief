title: MEXC shuts its Visa-linked Global Card weeks before FinCEN order takes effect
time: 2026-11-18T21:25:00+09:00
category: Exchanges
important: false
---
MEXC discontinued its Global Card on Nov. 18, less than three months after launching the Visa-linked USDT spending card on Aug. 31, and blamed changes at an unnamed third-party card issuer. Remaining card balances will be snapshotted on Nov. 21 and moved to users' spot accounts. The shutdown comes as FinCEN's order naming MEXC a primary money laundering concern is set to take effect on Dec. 10.
