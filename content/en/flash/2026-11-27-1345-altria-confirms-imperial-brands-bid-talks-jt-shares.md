Marlboro maker Altria confirmed it is considering an offer for Britain's Imperial Brands and is in talks with another party it did not name.

Bloomberg reported that the partner is Japan Tobacco and that the two are studying a joint all-cash deal. JT shares rose more than 4% in Tokyo on Friday, a day after Imperial gained about 13% in London.
