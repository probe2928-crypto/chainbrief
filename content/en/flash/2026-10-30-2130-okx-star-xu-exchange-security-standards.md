OKX founder Star Xu called on exchange chiefs to agree common security standards, arguing safety should not be a field for competition. His proposals include real-time settlement between verified exchanges, added checks and delays for large withdrawals to unknown addresses or smart-contract approvals enforced at the signing layer, and joint incident alerts and asset freezes.
