title: Binance hackers minted six NEAR memecoins to dress up $430,000 as trading gains, Elliptic says
time: 2026-11-10T21:44:00+09:00
category: Exchanges
important: false
---
Launderers linked to North Korea created at least six throwaway memecoins on NEAR and traded them to make about 117,000 stolen NEAR, worth roughly $430,000, look like trading profit, blockchain analytics firm Elliptic said. The amount is small next to the roughly $6.3 billion taken from Binance on Oct. 29, but it shows the attackers testing new ways to move the funds. [Source: Elliptic](https://www.elliptic.co/insights/north-korea-meme-coins-binance-hack/)
