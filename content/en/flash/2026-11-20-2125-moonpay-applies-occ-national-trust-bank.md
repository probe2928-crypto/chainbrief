title: MoonPay applies to the OCC for a national trust bank charter ahead of GENIUS Act deadline
time: 2026-11-20T21:25:00+09:00
category: US
important: false
---
Payments firm MoonPay has asked the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency to charter MoonPay National Trust Bank, an uninsured trust bank that would handle custody and stablecoin issuance, redemption and reserve management. The application is dated Nov. 17 and was logged by the OCC a day later. MoonPay already holds a New York trust charter, and the GENIUS Act's licensing requirement for stablecoin issuers takes effect on Jan. 18, 2027.
