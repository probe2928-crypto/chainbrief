Brent crude futures settled $1.96 higher at $96.57 a barrel on Thursday after Iran's Revolutionary Guards said two tankers struck sea mines north of Oman's Khasab port while crossing the Strait of Hormuz.

The Guards said both ships caught fire and that one was a supertanker carrying more than 2 million barrels of crude; no independent confirmation of the damage has been published.

WTI did not settle because of the US Thanksgiving holiday and traded around $84.80 in the afternoon, ahead of the OPEC+ meeting on Nov. 29.
