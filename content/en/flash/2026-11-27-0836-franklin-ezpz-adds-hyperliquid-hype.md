Franklin Templeton's Cboe-listed Franklin Crypto Index ETF (EZPZ) will start holding Hyperliquid's HYPE token on Dec. 1, taking the fund to eight assets.

The change follows CF Benchmarks' quarterly reconstitution of the index the fund tracks, which adds HYPE without dropping any existing constituent.

A prospectus supplement filed with the SEC says EZPZ will hold bitcoin, ether, XRP, Solana, Dogecoin, Cardano, Stellar and HYPE in roughly the same weights as the index; it gives no separate figure for HYPE's share.
