title: CFTC warns Kalshi of possible charges over how it notified new contracts, Politico reports
time: 2026-11-11T21:31:00+09:00
category: US
important: false
---
The Commodity Futures Trading Commission has privately told Kalshi it may face charges over whether the prediction market properly notified the agency about dozens of new contracts, Politico reported Tuesday, citing a person familiar with the matter. No charges have been filed, and the CFTC has not commented publicly. A Kalshi spokesperson called it a procedural error involving a handful of contracts, many of which never went live, and said the company is already fixing it.
