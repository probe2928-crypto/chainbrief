important: false
---
Gemini Space Station reported third-quarter revenue of $40.9 million on Thursday, down 19% from a year earlier, with a net loss of $58.6 million, or 46 cents a share, against $159.5 million a year ago; trading volume shrank to $4.5 billion from $16.4 billion. Hours earlier, CoinDesk reported that Gemini had sold its UK and European operations to Singapore-based stablecoin payments firm KAST for less than $10 million, a deal Gemini declined to comment on. The exchange had said in February it would wind down those businesses and keep the U.S. and Singapore.
