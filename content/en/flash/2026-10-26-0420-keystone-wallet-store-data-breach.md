title: Keystone discloses breach of hardware wallet store's customer data
time: 2026-10-26T04:20:00+09:00
category: On-chain
important: false
---
Hardware wallet maker Keystone said a third party obtained customer names, contact details, shipping addresses and order information through its Shopify-based online store. The company said private keys, recovery phrases and payment details were not exposed, and it warned users about scammers posing as Keystone. It detected the access on Oct. 20 and disclosed it publicly on Oct. 24, but has not said how many customers were affected.
