BlackRock's iShares Bitcoin Trust lost about $213 million on Monday, Nov. 9, its seventh consecutive session of net redemptions since Oct. 30, bringing the streak to roughly $1.66 billion, per Farside data cited by Cointelegraph and TokenPost. All U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs together shed about $201 million that day, and the group is now about $1.1 billion in net outflows for 2026. [Source: Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/markets/blackrock-ibit-bitcoin-etf-outflows)
