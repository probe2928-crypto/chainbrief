title: Monad's one-year cliff releases more than 16 billion MON on Nov. 24
time: 2026-11-23T09:34:00+09:00
category: Altcoins
important: false
---
About 16.6 billion MON tokens unlock on Tuesday, Nov. 24, one year after Monad's mainnet launch, according to CryptoRank; DefiLlama records about 17.1 billion. Roughly 10.7 billion go to the team, 4.9 billion to early investors and 1 billion to the Category Labs treasury, a release about 1.4 times the tokens now circulating, per DefiLlama figures cited by Coinranking and Altcoin Buzz. Dollar estimates range from about $216 million to $324 million depending on when the price was taken.
