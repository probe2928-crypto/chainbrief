Binance said on Thursday its withdrawal recovery work is complete and the suspension imposed after the October security incident has been fully lifted.

Withdrawals froze on Oct. 29 after attackers took about $6.3 billion; the exchange reopened them in stages from Nov. 1, starting with bitcoin, and had 87 more networks running by Nov. 14.

Binance says it covered the losses with more than $6.34 billion of its own funds and plans to rebuild its user protection fund to about $1 billion. A handful of networks remain paused for maintenance or planned upgrades unrelated to the hack.
