title: Coinbase adjusts offline key storage as rivals react to Binance breach
time: 2026-10-31T21:25:00+09:00
category: Exchanges
important: false
---
Coinbase said it had changed the systems it uses to hold crypto keys offline, describing the move as purely precautionary after the theft at Binance. Kraken said it was studying the incident for lessons for its own defenses, and OKX chief executive Star Xu called for a real-time alert system so exchanges can share attack data and freeze suspected stolen funds quickly. [Source: Reuters via The Business Standard](https://www.tbsnews.net/world/crypto-exchanges-call-quick-moves-bolster-defenses-after-binance-heist-1588396)
