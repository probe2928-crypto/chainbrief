🇺🇸 **US**
- Wall Street returns from Thanksgiving for a half-day. Stocks close early at 1 p.m. ET.
- The USTR's tariff plan for 14 economies is now on paper. Japan, the EU and South Korea get rates of up to 15%, in line with existing deals, with higher fallback rates if talks are judged unsatisfactory.
- Comments are due Jan. 5 and hearings start Jan. 25, so final action is unlikely before late January. Nothing changes at the border yet.

🇪🇺 **Europe**
- Eurozone governments agreed to back Spain's Pablo Hernández de Cos as the next ECB president. Read as a vote for continuity, it helped lift European stocks across the board alongside relief that the EU tariff rate was capped at the deal level.
- Imperial Brands jumped more than 13% after Altria said it was weighing an all-cash offer for the UK tobacco group.

🌏 **Asia**
- Japan: Tokyo's November CPI is out at 8:30 a.m. local time. Economists expect core inflation to climb to around 3%, a key input before the Bank of Japan meets Dec. 17-18.
- China: October industrial profits are due today. Beijing faces a proposed US rate of up to 20%, with a fallback of up to 40%.
- Korea: its base rate matches last year's 15% deal, but a fallback of up to 25% hangs over the January hearings.

₿ **Crypto**
- Bitcoin bounced while US markets were shut, with short sellers forced out of their positions.
- US spot bitcoin ETFs lost $453 million on Nov. 25, per Farside. November outflows have passed $5 billion, the biggest month on record.
- Tonight's flows are the first post-holiday read on whether institutions are still selling into the bounce.
