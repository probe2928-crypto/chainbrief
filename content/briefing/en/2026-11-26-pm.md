Starting today, CHAINBRIEF covers US and Asian markets alongside crypto. The global briefing lands twice a day, before Asia opens and before Wall Street opens.

🇺🇸 **US**
- Wall Street is shut for Thanksgiving and reopens Friday for a shortened session.
- Hotter-than-expected inflation data hit tech on Wednesday, and traders are now openly talking about a December Fed hike rather than a cut.
- The Fed's Beige Book: 8 of 12 districts saw slight-to-moderate growth, 4 were flat, and prices rose moderately. [Beige Book](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/beige-book-default.htm)
- Washington proposed new tariffs on 14 economies including China, the EU, Japan and South Korea. Tokyo called them "regrettable"; Beijing asked for talks.

🇪🇺 **Europe**
- The ECB publishes its October minutes today and the Bank of England its Financial Stability Report. BoE's Catherine Mann wants rates higher, and sooner.

🌏 **Asia**
- Japan: BoJ board member Tamura floated a quarter-point hike every few months. The yen is hovering near 159 per dollar.
- Korea: the Bank of Korea raised its policy rate to 3.25% from 3.00%, above 3% for the first time since November 2024, and lifted its 2027 growth forecast to 4.1% from 2.9% on chip exports.
- Hong Kong's Hang Seng and the Nikkei rose; Seoul bounced after a steep sell-off the day before.

🛢 **Middle East & commodities**
- Yemen's Houthis claimed strikes on Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura refinery and Jeddah port, putting supply risk back into oil.

₿ **Crypto**
- Bitcoin is holding around $60,000 after a drop of more than 20% this month, with US spot ETFs still seeing outflows.
- The House Financial Services Committee passed the Strategic Bitcoin Reserve bill 28–21. It would bar sales of federal bitcoin for at least 20 years; a full House vote is next.

📅 **Tonight**
- No major US data with markets closed. Watch the ECB minutes and the BoE report; OPEC+ meets Sunday, Nov 29.
